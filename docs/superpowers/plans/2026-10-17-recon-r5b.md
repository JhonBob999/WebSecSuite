**Architecture:** Keep Local Self-Test unchanged. A separate asynchronous DNS preflight issues a short-lived observation. A dedicated trusted adapter binds a one-shot approval to that observation, numeric IPv4, verified executable and immutable profile. A separate strict output validator feeds the existing atomic inventory importer.
